## What CI certifies

GitHub Actions certifies the current preferred 34-event and strict 29-event results, their explicit stacked datasets, event-time/balance/influence diagnostics, supporting annual regressions, the frozen 25-event benchmark, figure generation, and LaTeX compilation.

CI does **not** convert documentary or legal judgment into an automated fact. In particular, independent verification of the nine second-pass documentary classifications remains a pre-submission human safeguard, and the row-level source for the original provision-coding appendix should be preserved if that appendix is to be advertised as fully computationally reproducible.
