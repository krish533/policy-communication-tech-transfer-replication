\begin{abstract}
\noindent What happens when a university rewrites its intellectual-property policy? We follow technology-transfer outcomes around documented policy revisions and compare rewrites whose written stance toward inventors shifts in different directions. Outcomes do diverge after a rewrite, but mostly in licensing, not across the whole path from invention to commercialization. Universities choose how they revise their policies, so we see this as a pattern that needs explaining, not evidence that the wording itself drives the change.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} university technology transfer; intellectual-property policy; licensing; policy communication; policy revisions; inventor incentives.\\
